merge_patterns cheat sheet (convert.py --merged)
=================================================

Block keys (merge_patterns: [ ... ])
  patterns: "1-4"              reference MOD patterns in hex; ranges and commas ("0, 5-c"); a bare YAML number is decimal
  drop: [FM4, PSG1]            these channels' notes are left out in the block's patterns
  groups:                      the folds and moves that hold in those patterns

Group keys (merge_patterns groups and song-wide merge: groups)
  primary: FM5                 the channel that carries the fold: its column, its fill, its vibrato
  followers: [FM3, FM4, PSG1]  folded onto the primary as composites or solo notes
  mod_channel: 2  |  FM2       merge_patterns only: the column the primary's notes take here (a channels: number or a source);
                               free there = its owner folded, dropped or moved away; with no followers = a plain move
  cut_primary: true            a follower note over the primary's tail plays and cuts it (the drum rule)
  bank: true                   the group's mixes (not chip composites) share slots as sample banks with 9xx; any primary
                               (a melodic note gives its attack row to 9xx)
  bank_drums: true             a drum primary's lone hits go into the banks too (each drum a sound): its drums'
                               slots go to composites; implies bank, needs no followers
  mix_note: C3                 cap on the note a mix is made at (bytes vs the hat's treble; C2 loses the hat, F2 keeps it to 5.5 kHz)
  mix_at: primary              a mix is made at the primary's own note, so a looped lead/bass/chord keeps its loop
                               (a few KB instead of the whole note unrolled); the chime is resampled down into it
  max_composites: 8            keep the N most-played composites; the rest use a stand-in or the primary alone
  fill_lost: true              follower notes the fold cannot place go to the fill pool (any silent column)
  fill_cut: true               follower notes the fold would cut short go to the pool, whole notes only
  fill: true                   merge_patterns only, no followers, no mod_channel: the channel's notes are sprinkled over
                               whichever column in use is silent when each starts; lost where none is
  cut_after: 8                 merge_patterns only: a pooled note may take this group's column once its note is N ticks old
  fm_on_chip: false            default true: a mix's FM primary + FM followers render together on the chip, the rest mixed on top;
                               false sums the looped samples
  loop_mix: true               a long mix loops where its sum settles (lossy; for a pitched primary)
  limit_db: 3                  a mix past full scale has its peaks limited by up to N dB instead of being turned down
  loop_drift_db / loop_min_ms / loop_start_ms / loop_decay: slide   the group's chip composites' sustain loops
                               (loop_min_ms / loop_start_ms its looped mixes' too); default the primary's entry's, then the song's
  treble_shelf_db: 3           a brightness shelf on the group's composites, over the song's; treble_shelf_hz its corner
  dither: shaped | flat | off  the composites' quantisation (default the primary's entry's, then samples.dither)
  name: "chords"               the composites' sample name (the note each plays at is added)

Song level (beside the blocks)
  merge_bank_slots: auto       default auto: the converter picks the slots held back for the banks (rebuilds once);
                               a number pins it, and the report says when one went unused or a sound found no bank
  merge_tolerance: 1           ticks a follower note-on may be off the primary's and still fold (default 1)
  merge_fill: [PSG1]           pooled channels: each note on whichever output column is silent
  merge_fill_cut_after: {DAC: 2}   song-wide fallback for cut_after, by the column's owner
  merge_drop: [FM3]            channels left out of the merged build everywhere
  merge_twins: always          default short: same-shape composites give up their slot only while slots are short
  merge_max_synth_shift: 0     cap on how far above its root a sample is rendered (default 12; 0 halves shifted samples)
  merge_loop_timbre: true      the merged build's loops wait for the timbre to hold too (bigger samples; default off)
  loop_drift_db: 6             this song's loop_drift_db over settings.yaml's (1): loops may freeze that far above
                               the settled level, so looped samples end sooner (both builds)
  treble_shelf_db: 2           this song's shelf over settings.yaml's (0; both builds)
  merge_output_file: output/x.mod   where --merged writes (default <output>_merged.mod)
  dac_samples: merge_saturate_db    a drum's saturate_db in the merged build only

Gotcha
  every group starts with "- " — without it its keys merge into the group above (the loader now refuses the duplicate key)

Tools (each takes --variant NAME; fold_csv --write does not)
  python tools/fold_csv.py <config> <table.csv> --write --bank --mix-note C3   draft the section from a fold table
                                                                                (--bank / --mix-note: on drum-primary groups)
  python tools/merge_survey.py <config> --all                                   every channel pair, not only the clean ones
  python convert.py <config> --merged                                           build; read "composite slots" and the warnings
  python tools/mod_audit.py output/<song>_merged.mod --banks                    samples vs the notes that play them; per 9xx sound
